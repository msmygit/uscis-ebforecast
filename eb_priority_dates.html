How the estimate works

Queue method. People ahead of your priority date (pending I-485 inventory, plus approved petitions that have not yet filed) divided by the visas the law makes available to your category and country each year. Early / base / late cases vary family size, drop-out, and how many unused numbers spill over from other countries.

Limits. The inventory excludes consular cases, people yet to file, and masks counts of 1–10 (“D”, treated as 5). India EB-2/EB-3 inventory stops at early 2015, so later India dates lean on petition totals. Dependents are not stated in the inventory. Annual limits and supply assumptions are listed below and can be changed in the command-line tool.

Not legal advice. Dates move with demand, policy and law. Treat this as a planning range, not a prediction.
